Lumrivo Clinical

Python + SQLiteSpeech-to-text + LLM drafting32 integration testsRole-based access

A consultation-to-prescription workspace for multi-doctor hospitals. The doctor records the consultation with consent. AI transcribes it and drafts a structured record: complaints, history, allergies, assessment, medicines, tests, diet and follow-up. The doctor reviews and approves, then prints a prescription and a plain-language care sheet. The next visit shows the full history.

71-second captioned walkthrough on fictional patients. The recording uses the app's scripted example so it runs without a key; with an OpenAI key set, the same screen transcribes audio and generates the draft.

Where the AI sits

  • Audio → transcript (gpt-4o-transcribe)
  • Transcript → strict-JSON draft (gpt-4.1-mini)
  • Every medicine keeps the sentence it came from
  • AI proposes; it can never approve or print

What I chose to cut

  • Automatic prescribing or dosing suggestions
  • Patient phone numbers on printed documents
  • Real-patient use until clinical validation

Build 2 · built in about a month, shipping weekly

Lumrivo Travel Ops

Next.js + PostgreSQLMulti-tenant SaaSLLM intake + itinerary writingLive in production

An operations system for Australian multi-day tour operators who sell direct and through travel agents. It runs the whole trip lifecycle: enquiry, quote, booking, payments, supplier requests, changes and cancellations.

57-second captioned walkthrough recorded on the live system with test bookings.

WhatsApp / email / web form → AI extracts name, dates, party, room type → staff confirm → priced quote → customer itinerary link → booking with seat hold

Where the AI sits

  • Pasted emails and WhatsApp messages become structured enquiries; missing details are flagged, never guessed
  • Drafts the day-by-day itinerary text for the customer
  • Staff approve every AI output before it is used

Hard rules in the database

  • Departures can never be oversold, even under concurrent bookings
  • Every company's data is isolated
  • Edits are version-checked; every change is logged
